    How to play
    • Start with fish. Trade around the market and finish with as many fish as you can.
    • Each stall offers a fixed trade. Tap it once to trade one whole lot.
    • Every stall has limited stock. The dots and “trades left” count it down.
    • Some markets limit your basket. Bread, salt and wool take space; fish do not.
    • Mixed offers need every ingredient together. “3 bread + 2 salt” means both, not either.
    • Only fish count at closing. Leftover goods are worth nothing.
    • Undo and Reset are free. Closing reveals the best score; the walkthrough is optional.
    • Your first result is saved. Play again as often as you like; replays are labeled.
